What this study was about

You used two different ways of telling a recommender what you like: repeated pairwise choices, and ranking ten films at a time. Both feed the same Plackett–Luce preference model, so the only thing that differed was how you were asked.

The real question is which method captures more of your taste per minute of your time — a ranking gives more information per task, but takes far longer than a single click. The ratings you gave near the end were held out to score both.

No study is actually being run here: this is the interface and protocol for one. Your answers live in a server-side session for as long as this browser session lasts, are shown back to you above, and are not stored anywhere else.
